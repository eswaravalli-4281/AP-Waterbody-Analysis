# 🌊 AP District-Wise Water Body Analysis using Data Mining

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes the **Andhra Pradesh Water Body Census** dataset using Python and data mining techniques to evaluate water body performance, identify regional disparities, and uncover actionable insights for sustainable water resource management. The analysis integrates **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**, **Feature Engineering**, **K-Means Clustering**, **Decision Tree (CART)**, and **Apriori Association Rule Mining** to classify water bodies and discover hidden patterns within the data.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze the distribution and characteristics of water bodies across Andhra Pradesh.
* Classify water bodies into **High-Value**, **Underutilized**, and **Critical** performance categories.
* Identify the key factors influencing water body performance.
* Discover hidden relationships between governance, usage, and maintenance practices.
* Generate data-driven insights to support effective water resource planning and decision-making.

---

## 📊 Dataset

* **Source:** First Census of Water Bodies, Government of India
* **Region:** Andhra Pradesh
* **Original Dataset Size:** ~164,000+ water body records

> **Note:** This repository contains a representative sample of the original dataset due to GitHub file size limitations. The complete dataset is publicly available through the Government of India's Open Government Data Portal.

## 📈 Key Findings

* Analyzed **164K+** water body records to understand district-wise water resource distribution across Andhra Pradesh.
* Performed comprehensive analysis of water body count, storage capacity, irrigation potential, water body types, and population benefited.
* Engineered domain-specific features including **Efficiency Ratio**, **Loss Percentage**, and **People Benefited** to evaluate water body performance.
* Classified water bodies into **High-Value**, **Underutilized**, and **Critical** categories using **K-Means Clustering**.
* Identified that **75%+** of water bodies are underutilized, highlighting significant opportunities for improved utilization and management.
* Identified districts with a higher concentration of critical water bodies requiring priority intervention and districts with strong-performing water resources.
* Applied a **Decision Tree (CART)** model to identify the most influential factors affecting water body performance and generate interpretable decision rules.
* Used the **Apriori Algorithm** to discover hidden relationships between governance practices, monitoring status, usage patterns, and water body performance.
* Generated actionable insights to support evidence-based policy decisions and sustainable water resource management.
